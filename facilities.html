Get the right momentum for your workday with our fully equipped on-site gym and wellness center. Access premium cardio machines, free weights, and dedicated yoga spaces anytime. Recharge fast with certified trainers and refresh in private shower suites before your next sprint session.

Step into our high-tech eSports gaming lounge complete with 4K HDR consoles, ultra-wide PC rigs, and VR setups. Whether you're competing in team tournaments or taking a quick 15-minute lunch break, it's designed to build cross-team friendships.

Fuel your innovation at our full-service gourmet tech café. Featuring micro-batch espresso beans, organic teas, and complimentary chef-prepared lunches, our lounge provides the perfect backdrop for spontaneous brainstorming sessions.
